About this assistant

How your answers are produced

  1. Search. Your question is matched against the approved IIM knowledge base.
  2. Ground. Only reliable matches are used. High-risk facts such as fees use checked templates.
  3. Answer. A local Ollama model writes the reply from those sources, which are shown under each answer.
  4. Fallback. If nothing reliable is found, you're pointed to an official IIM contact instead of a guess.

The status badge in the header shows whether the AI model is online. If it is offline, rule-based answers are still available.
